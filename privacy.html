ProSe Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

ProSe Pro ("the app") is developed and operated by ProsePro. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What the app does

ProSe Pro lets you upload a legal document, receive an AI-generated analysis of it, generate a drafted response or motion, and test that draft against an AI adversary. ProSe Pro provides general legal information only. It is not a law firm, and nothing in the app is legal advice or creates an attorney-client relationship.

Information you provide

Documents you upload. When you choose a document to analyze (PDF, DOCX, or TXT), the file is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, where its text is extracted and processed to produce your analysis. Uploaded files are processed to provide the service and are not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

Text you enter. Text you type or generate in the drafting and adversary features is sent to our server so the AI can work on it.

AI processing. Document text and drafting text are processed using Anthropic's Claude API, which acts as our AI service provider. That processing is performed on our behalf to return results to you.

Purchases. Subscriptions and credit packs are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we do not receive or store your card number. We receive purchase confirmations so the app can unlock paid features.

Information the app does not collect

No user accounts and no sign-in: the app does not ask for your name, email address, or a password. No advertising and no ad identifiers. No analytics or crash-reporting services. No location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. Usage counters for the free allowance are stored only on your device.

Retention

Documents and text are processed to generate your results. We do not build profiles of users, and we do not keep uploaded documents to train models.

Children

ProSe Pro is intended for adults (18+) dealing with legal matters. It is not directed to children.

Changes
